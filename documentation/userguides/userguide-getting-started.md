# Getting Started

**Audience:** Someone playing or running a game with Coffee Pub Scribe.

The first five minutes: what Scribe needs, what changes on screen when you enable it, and how to get
one passage in front of your players. Each feature has its own guide, linked at the end.

## What Scribe is for

You have written a scene in a journal entry -- a description of a room, an overheard conversation, a
piece of read-aloud text. Scribe gives you a one-click way to put that passage in front of your
players: as a card in the chat log, as a handout they can open later, or as a file you can keep
outside Foundry.

## What you need

Foundry VTT version 13, and **Coffee Pub Blacksmith**, which is required. Scribe will not work
without it. Blacksmith also supplies the chat card styling, so the colour of your narration cards is
chosen there rather than here.

Enable both in your world's module settings.

## What changes on screen

Two things, and both are easy to miss until you look for them.

**A toolbar appears on blockquotes in journal pages.** Only a GM sees it. Players see the journal
exactly as they did before. If your journals contain no blockquotes yet, nothing appears anywhere.

**A button appears in the title bar of every journal window**, next to the close control, for opening
the whole journal in a browser tab.

## Share your first passage

1. Open a journal page and click Edit.
2. Put the text you want to share inside a blockquote. In Foundry's editor that is the quote button
   on the formatting toolbar.
3. Save the page.
4. A toolbar appears along the bottom of the block. Click **Narration**.

The passage is posted to the chat log as a card everyone at the table can read. That is the whole
core of the module.
