# Sharing to Chat

**Audience:** A GM running a game with Coffee Pub Scribe.

Putting a passage, or a single picture from it, into the chat log where the whole table sees it.

## Send a passage

Click **Narration** on the blockquote's toolbar. The passage is posted to the chat log immediately,
as a card everyone can read. There is no confirmation step and no preview, so read the passage before
you click.

Everything you put in the block comes across, laid out according to the headings and dialogue markers
described in [Writing a scene](userguide-writing-a-scene.md). The toolbar itself is stripped out --
players never see it.

**Everyone sees it, including players.** This is not a whisper. There is no way to send a narration
card to one player.

## What players can do with it

Read it, and click any document links inside it that they have permission to open. The card sits in
the chat log like any other message and scrolls away with it -- if you want the passage to stay
available, use [Handouts](userguide-handouts.md) instead.

## Send a single picture

Click **Illustration** to post one image from the passage to chat on its own, without the surrounding
text. You get one Illustration button per image in the block, in the order the images appear.

The card carries the picture and a **View Illustration** button. Anyone at the table can click that
button to open the image at full size in its own window, scaled to fit their screen. It works for
players as well as the GM, and it keeps working after someone reloads their browser.

Use this when you want the table looking at a picture rather than reading a description of it.

## Choosing the colour

All Scribe cards take their colour from the **Card Style** setting, which lists themes supplied by
Blacksmith. Leaving it on Blacksmith Default keeps Scribe's cards matching every other Coffee Pub
card in the world.

Changing it affects cards posted afterwards. Cards already in the log keep the colour they were
posted with.

## If a card looks wrong

Scribe no longer styles the chat log itself -- Blacksmith owns how the card looks. If a card renders
with no styling at all, the likeliest cause is that Blacksmith is not enabled, which also disables
most of the rest of Scribe.
