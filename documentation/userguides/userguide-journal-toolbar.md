# The Journal Toolbar

**Audience:** A GM running a game with Coffee Pub Scribe.

The bar of buttons Scribe puts on journal blockquotes: when it appears, who can see it, and what each
button does.

## When it appears

Scribe adds a toolbar to the bottom of **every blockquote on a journal page**. There is nothing to
switch on per passage -- writing a blockquote is the whole trigger.

Two consequences worth knowing before you start:

- **If you use blockquotes for ordinary pull quotes**, Scribe puts a toolbar on those too. There is
  no way to exclude one; turning off the toolbar turns it off everywhere.
- **Nothing appears while you are editing.** A toolbar inserted into a live editor would be saved
  into the page, so Scribe waits until you leave edit mode. Save the page to see it.

The toolbar may take a second to appear after a page opens, and reappears by itself after you switch
pages or the journal re-renders.

## Who sees it

**Only a GM.** Players see the journal exactly as they did before, with no toolbar and no indication
one exists.

The button in the journal's title bar is separate and has its own audience -- see
[Exporting and printing](userguide-export.md).

## The buttons

Buttons appear left to right in this order, and each can be turned off individually in the settings.
Turn all of them off and the toolbar disappears rather than leaving an empty bar.

**Export** saves the journal page as an HTML file on your computer.

**Copy** puts the passage's HTML on your clipboard, without the toolbar, ready to paste into another
page.

**Handout** copies the passage into a journal entry the players can open, and posts a link to chat.
See [Handouts](userguide-handouts.md).

**Illustration** posts one image from the passage to chat on its own. **You get one of these per
image in the block**, so a passage with three pictures has three Illustration buttons, in the order
the images appear.

**Narration** posts the whole passage to chat as a card. See
[Sharing to chat](userguide-sharing-to-chat.md).

## Making the toolbar narrower

Each button shows its name beside its icon by default. Turn off **Show Button Labels** in the
settings and you get icons alone, which matters most on a passage with several images, where the
Illustration buttons multiply.

## If the toolbar does not appear

Check, in order: that you are logged in as a GM; that you have saved the page and are not still in
the editor; that **Toolbar Enabled** is on; and that at least one of the five button settings is on.
